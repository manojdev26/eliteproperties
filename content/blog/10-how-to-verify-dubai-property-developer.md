# How to Verify a Dubai Property Developer, Broker and Escrow Account

Every off-plan purchase reaches a moment where you transfer a significant amount of money to a company you have often never met, for a property that does not yet exist.

Dubai's regulatory framework is built to make that safe. The framework only protects you if you use it, and most buyers never run the checks that take twenty minutes and remove nearly all of the risk.

This is the checklist we would want a member of our own family to follow, including when dealing with us.

**How do you verify a Dubai property developer or broker?** Check the brokerage on the Dubai Land Department licensed-brokers register using its Office Registration Number, confirm the individual agent's Broker Registration Number, and verify the project's Trakheesi advertising permit. Before paying, confirm the escrow account named in your Sale and Purchase Agreement and never transfer funds to a personal account.

## Running the Checks

### 1. Verify the brokerage

Look up the firm on the [Dubai Land Department](https://dubailand.gov.ae/) licensed-brokers register using its ORN. Confirm the legal name matches the entity on your paperwork exactly. "Elite Global Properties L.L.C" and a similarly named company are not the same thing, and impersonation of established brands is a known tactic.

### 2. Verify the individual agent

Ask for the BRN of the person you are dealing with and check it. A licensed firm can still have unregistered people making calls.

### 3. Verify the advertising permit

Legitimate Dubai property advertising carries a Trakheesi permit number. Its absence on a listing is a meaningful signal.

### 4. Verify the developer and the project

Confirm the developer is registered and the project is a registered development with escrow arrangements in place. For off-plan, purchases are recorded through the Oqood system, which registers your interest in the unit before the final title deed is issued at handover.

### 5. Verify the escrow account

The most important check of all. For off-plan purchases, payments are made only into the developer's RERA-regulated escrow account named in your Sale and Purchase Agreement.

Before transferring, confirm the account details in the SPA itself rather than in an email, and confirm the account name matches the developer. Funds in escrow are released against construction progress rather than handed over as a lump sum.

## Warning Signs

Most property fraud is unsophisticated. It relies on speed and enthusiasm rather than technical skill.

**A request to pay a personal account.** There is no legitimate version of this. We will never ask you to transfer funds to a personal account. If anyone does, stop.

**Changed payment details by email.** A common attack sends "updated" bank details late in the process. Always re-verify against the SPA and by phone on a number you already had.

**Pressure to pay before documentation.** Genuine launches do move quickly, and that is not the same as being told to transfer before seeing the reservation form and payment schedule.

**Guaranteed returns.** Returns depend on the market and are not guaranteed. Any specific guaranteed yield on an off-plan building should be treated as a serious warning.

**No licence details.** If ORN, trade licence and permit numbers are not readily available, the conversation should end.

**Prices well below the developer's list.** On a current launch bought direct, you are buying at the developer's price list. A large discount usually means either a different unit than described or a party who does not hold what they are selling.

**Refusal to put costs in writing.** Every fee affecting your purchase should be documented before you commit.

## Read the SPA, Not the Brochure

Renders and imagery are illustrative and non-binding. The Sale and Purchase Agreement prevails in case of any discrepancy. That sentence appears on our own site because it is the single most useful thing a buyer can internalise.

In the SPA, read closely:

- The unit specification and area schedule
- The payment schedule and what triggers each instalment
- The escrow account details
- The expected handover date and delay provisions
- Conditions on resale before completion
- What happens if you miss a payment

If something in the SPA contradicts what you were told verbally, the SPA governs. Ask for the discrepancy to be explained before signing, not after.

## Questions Worth Asking Your Advisor

1. What is your ORN, and what is your BRN?
2. Is this a current launch sold direct from the developer?
3. What is the Trakheesi permit number for this project?
4. Which escrow account will my payments go into, and where is it named in the SPA?
5. What is the projected service charge per square foot?
6. What is the stated handover date, and what happens if it moves?
7. Can you put the full cost breakdown in writing?

Any advisor who is comfortable with these questions is probably worth working with. Any who is not has answered the question in a different way.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I check if a Dubai real estate agent is licensed?
Ask for the brokerage's Office Registration Number and the agent's Broker Registration Number, then verify them on the Dubai Land Department licensed-brokers register. Licensed firms publish these openly.

### What is a Trakheesi permit?
Trakheesi is the permit system governing property advertising in Dubai. Legitimate listings display a permit number, and its absence is a warning sign.

### What is an escrow account and why does it matter?
It is a regulated account holding off-plan buyer payments, with funds released to the developer against construction progress. It keeps your money tied to the project rather than to the developer's general operations.

### Should I ever pay a deposit to an agent's personal account?
No. Off-plan payments go only into the developer's RERA-regulated escrow account named in your Sale and Purchase Agreement. A request to pay a personal account should end the transaction.

### What is Oqood registration?
Oqood is the system used to register off-plan purchases before completion, recording your interest in the unit ahead of the title deed being issued at handover.

### What should I do if payment details change mid-purchase?
Treat it as suspicious. Re-verify against the SPA and confirm by phone using a number you already held, not one supplied in the new message.

### Can I verify a developer myself?
Yes. Developer and project registration details are held by the regulator, and your advisor should be able to point you to them. A reluctance to do so is itself informative.

### Is off-plan property in Dubai safe?
The regulatory framework, including escrow and permit requirements, is designed to protect buyers. It works when you verify the parties and the account before paying, and read the SPA before signing.
