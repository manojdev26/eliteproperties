# Can Foreigners Buy Property in Dubai? What Overseas Buyers Need to Know

Almost every first conversation we have starts with the same question. Someone has seen a residence they like, and before anything else they want to know whether they are actually allowed to own it.

The answer is yes, and it has been yes for over two decades. What matters is understanding exactly what you are buying, where you are allowed to buy it, and whose name ends up on the title.

**Can a foreigner buy property in Dubai?** Yes. Foreign nationals, whether resident in the UAE or living abroad, can buy freehold property in designated areas of Dubai and own it outright. The title is registered in the buyer's own name at the Dubai Land Department. There is no requirement to hold UAE residency, and no local partner or sponsor is needed.

That is the short version. The detail is where buyers get value.

## Freehold Versus Leasehold

Two forms of ownership are available to overseas buyers, and the difference is significant.

**Freehold** means you own the property and, in the case of a villa or townhouse, the land it sits on. Ownership has no expiry date. You can sell it, lease it, or pass it to your heirs. The title deed carries your name.

**Leasehold** grants you the right to use a property for a fixed term, commonly up to 99 years, while the underlying ownership stays with the freeholder. It is far less common in the new-build market that most international buyers are looking at.

At Elite Global, we present freehold residences only. Every project on our shortlist can be owned outright by a foreign investor, with the title registered at the Dubai Land Department. If a property cannot be owned that way, it does not go on the list.

## Where Foreigners Can Buy

Foreign ownership is permitted in areas the Dubai government has designated as freehold. These are not obscure corners of the city. They include most of the addresses international buyers already have in mind.

The communities we work in most often:

- **Palm Jumeirah**, beachfront living on the island itself
- **Dubai Hills Estate**, green and golf-side, close to the centre
- **Business Bay**, central and dense with branded-residence towers
- **The Oasis**, Emaar's lagoon-led villa community
- **Dubai Islands**, an emerging waterfront and second-home destination
- **Dubai Maritime City**, waterfront apartments in the Forest District

Abu Dhabi operates its own investment zones under separate rules. Communities such as Al Ghadeer and Sobha City in Abu Dhabi are open to foreign buyers on the same freehold basis.

Because designated areas are set by regulation and can be updated, we confirm the ownership status of the specific building or plot you are considering rather than relying on a general list.

## What You Actually Need to Buy

The paperwork is lighter than most buyers expect, particularly for an off-plan purchase direct from a developer.

For a typical off-plan reservation you will need:

1. A valid passport copy
2. Proof of address and basic source-of-funds documentation
3. A signed reservation form and the booking deposit
4. A Sale and Purchase Agreement, signed once the developer issues it

You do not need a UAE residence visa to buy. You do not need a UAE bank account to buy, though many owners open one later for convenience. Purchase costs, including the Dubai Land Department registration fee, are confirmed in writing before you commit anything.

## Where Your Money Goes

This is the part we ask every buyer to read carefully, because it is where the real risk sits in any off-plan market.

For off-plan purchases, payments go only into the developer's RERA-regulated escrow account, named in your Sale and Purchase Agreement. That account is regulated, and funds are released to the developer against construction progress rather than all at once.

A licensed brokerage will never ask you to transfer funds to a personal account. If anyone does, stop and verify before you pay. ## Ownership and Residency

Buying property and gaining residency are related but separate matters.

Property at or above the UAE Golden Visa investment level can qualify the owner, and immediate family, for long-term residency. It is not automatic for every purchase, and the qualifying threshold applies to the property value rather than to your deposit.

Rather than generalise, we confirm eligibility for the exact residence you are considering before you commit. If a residence does not qualify and residency is your goal, we will tell you plainly and show you what does.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to live in the UAE to buy property in Dubai?
No. Non-residents can buy freehold property in designated areas and own it outright. Many of our clients buy from the UK, Europe and elsewhere without relocating.

### Will the title deed be in my personal name?
Yes. For freehold purchases in designated areas, the title is registered in the buyer's own name at the Dubai Land Department. Purchases can also be structured in a company name where appropriate.

### Do I need a UAE bank account?
Not to complete a purchase. Payments can be made by international transfer to the developer's escrow account. Many owners open a local account afterwards for service charges and rental income.

### Can I get a mortgage as a foreign buyer?
Non-resident mortgages are available from several UAE banks, though terms differ from those offered to residents. For off-plan purchases, developer payment plans often remove the need for financing during construction.

### Is buying off-plan safe for an overseas buyer?
Off-plan purchases are governed by your Sale and Purchase Agreement, with payments held in a RERA-regulated escrow account and released against construction progress. Checking the developer's track record and the permit number matters as much as the building itself.

### Can I rent the property out after handover?
Yes. Freehold owners can lease their property. Short-term letting requires the appropriate permit, and individual communities may set their own rules, so it is worth confirming for the specific building.

### How long does the purchase take?
An off-plan reservation can be completed in days. The payment plan then runs to handover, which is set by the project's construction schedule and stated in your SPA.
